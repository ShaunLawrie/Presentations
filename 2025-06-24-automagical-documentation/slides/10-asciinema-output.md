# Asciinema Output

## Slide Details

**Title:** Step 5: Saving in Asciinema Format  
**Subtitle:** Creating portable, embeddable terminal recordings

### Content
- Recordings saved in asciinema format
- Asciinema: JSON-based terminal session recording format
- Benefits of asciinema:
  - Lightweight (text-based, not video)
  - Searchable and copyable text
  - Consistent playback across browsers
  - Customizable playback speed
  - Professional appearance
- Files can be embedded in web documentation
- Compatible with asciinema players and viewers

### Talking Notes
- The terminal recordings are saved in asciinema format
- Asciinema is a standard for terminal session recordings
- Unlike video recordings, these are text-based and lightweight
- Users can copy and paste text from the recordings
- The playback is consistent and doesn't depend on video codecs
- Files are much smaller than equivalent video recordings
- The format is widely supported and can be embedded in web pages
- This creates portable, professional-looking terminal demonstrations
- The recordings maintain full fidelity of the terminal experience
